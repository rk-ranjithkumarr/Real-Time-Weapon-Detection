ALERT BEHAVIOR
==============

1. A gun or knife is detected above the configured confidence threshold.
2. The red detection box and warning appear immediately.
3. The included emergency-style WAV alarm starts and LOOPS continuously.
4. The alarm keeps sounding for as long as a weapon remains detected.
5. When the detector sees no weapon, the alarm stops automatically.
6. One snapshot and one CSV event are saved when each new alert starts.

Windows uses the built-in winsound module, so there is no additional audio package.
